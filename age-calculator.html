Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. How does the chronological age calculator handle leap years and February 29?

Our Gregorian calendar algorithm checks every leap year (years divisible by 4, except century years not divisible by 400) and exact month lengths (28, 29, 30, or 31 days) to compute your exact age.

2. How can I check my age as of a future exam cutoff date?

Simply enter your Date of Birth in the first box and select the official closing date mentioned in the job or admission advertisement in the 'Calculate Age As Of Date' box.

3. Can I use this calculator to find the exact duration between two business contracts or invoices?

Yes! Enter the project start date in the first field and the completion or invoice due date in the second field to see the exact difference in years, months, weeks, and days.

4. Is my Date of Birth stored or shared with any server?

No. Just like every utility on Free-Invoice.store, all date calculations execute 100% locally inside your web browser.

5. Does this tool also show which day of the week I was born on?

Yes, the result card automatically displays the exact day of the week you were born (e.g., Monday, Friday) alongside a live countdown to your next birthday.
