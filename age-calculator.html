How to Calculate Your Exact Chronological Age Online

Whether you are applying for a competitive civil service examination (CSS, PPSC, FPSC, UPSC), verifying admission eligibility, or calculating exact date intervals between contracts, our free online age calculator computes your exact age in years, months, days, weeks, and hours from your Date of Birth.

Preparing an Official Online Application?

Most government and university portals require verifying your exact age on the application closing date, uploading a 50KB passport photo (use our Free Image Reducer in KB), and submitting a personal statement within word limits (check our Word Counter).

How the Date of Birth Calculator Works

  1. Select your birth date in the Date of Birth field.
  2. Keep the second date set to today, or pick a specific cutoff date mentioned in an official job or admission notice.
  3. Review your exact Years, Months, Days, Total Weeks, Total Days, and Next Birthday Countdown immediately.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. Does this calculator account for leap years (29 days in February)?

Yes, our Gregorian calendar logic accounts for every leap year and varying month lengths (28, 29, 30, and 31 days) automatically.

2. Can I use this tool to find the exact duration between two business dates?

Yes! Enter your project start date in the first box and due date in the second box, then generate your client bill on our Free Invoice Generator.
