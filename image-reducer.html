How to Use (Quality Kaise Bachayein? — Golden Rule for 50KB Photos)

Agar aap apni photo ka size 50KB ya 100KB tak lana chahte hain aur chahte hain ke photo bilkul saaf (clear) rahe aur phate nahi, toh in steps ko follow karein:

  1. Upload your image using the box above.
  2. If the image resolution is very high (e.g., 3000x4000 pixels), lower the Width (px) box to 600px or 800px first (or click the ~50 KB preset).
  3. Leave the compression slider around 70% or 80%. Your image will stay clear and sharp while dropping below 50KB immediately.
