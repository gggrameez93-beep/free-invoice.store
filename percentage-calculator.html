Mastering Inclusive vs. Exclusive GST, Retail Discounts & Percentage Change

Whether you are preparing a commercial tax bill on our Free Invoice Generator, calculating profit margins for an online store, or filling out your academic percentage alongside your Exact Age Calculator results and 50KB Passport Photo, accurate percentage calculation prevents costly mistakes.

  • Adding Exclusive GST/VAT: Multiply your base price by (Tax Rate / 100) to get the tax amount, then add it to the base price.
  • Extracting Inclusive GST/VAT: Divide the gross total by (1 + Tax Rate / 100) to find the true pre-tax net price.
  • Calculating Percentage Growth: Subtract the old value from the new value, divide by the old value, and multiply by 100.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. How do I extract GST when the tax is already included in the total price?

Enter the total amount in the Base Amount field, type your GST rate (such as 18%), and switch the Calculation Mode dropdown to 'Tax Inclusive (Extract Tax)'. The tool will display the exact pre-tax Net Amount and Tax Amount.

2. How do I calculate my Matric or FSc marks percentage?

In Formula #2 ('X is what percent of Y?'), enter your obtained marks in the first box (e.g., 840) and total marks in the second box (e.g., 1100) to see your exact percentage (76.36%).

3. Can I use these figures on a printable PDF invoice?

Yes! Once you verify your net amount, discount, and tax rate here, open our Free Online Invoice Generator on the homepage to export a signed A4 PDF bill.

4. Are my financial numbers stored on your servers?

No. Every calculation executes 100% locally in your browser using client-side JavaScript.

5. Does this calculator support decimal tax rates like 16.5% or 8.25%?

Yes, both the GST calculator and the 3-in-1 percentage solver support decimal values for any regional sales tax or discount rate.
