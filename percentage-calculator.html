How to Calculate Percentages, Discounts & GST Accurately

Whether you are calculating inclusive or exclusive GST for a client bill on our Free Invoice Generator, checking retail discounts, or working out exam marks percentages, our online percentage calculator delivers instant results without manual formula errors.

Core Percentage & Tax Formulas

  • Exclusive Tax: Tax = (Net Price × Tax Rate) / 100.
  • Inclusive Tax Extraction: Net Price = Total Price / (1 + (Tax Rate / 100)).
  • Exam Marks Percentage: (Obtained Marks / Total Marks) × 100.
