Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. Do QR codes generated on Free-Invoice.store ever expire?

No. Because our tool generates direct static QR codes on the client side without routing through paid redirect servers, your QR codes work permanently with unlimited scans.

2. Can I add my downloaded QR code to an invoice?

Yes! Download your QR code as a PNG and upload it in the Company Logo or Signature slot on our Free Online Invoice Generator so clients can scan and pay immediately.

3. What color contrast works best for scanning QR codes?

Always choose a dark foreground color (such as navy, black, or deep teal) on a light background (white) so smartphone cameras can read the barcode in any lighting.

4. Are the links I enter stored on your database?

Never. All QR encoding happens locally inside your web browser.

5. Can I compress the downloaded QR PNG to under 50KB?

Yes, if an online portal requires an ultra-lightweight barcode image, open our Image Reducer in KB tool to convert or resize it in seconds.
