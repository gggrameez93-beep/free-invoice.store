Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. How are reading time and speaking time calculated?

Reading time is based on an average silent reading speed of 200 words per minute, while speaking time uses the standard presentation rate of 130 words per minute.

2. Is my text saved or uploaded anywhere?

No. All text counting and formatting runs locally in your browser memory and is never sent to any external server.
